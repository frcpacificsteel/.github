# Pacific Steel 5025

**FIRST Robotics Competition team 5025**<br>
Cathedral Catholic High School · San Diego, California

We are Pacific Steel, a student-built robotics team that designs, builds, programs, and competes with a new robot every FRC season. The work is hands-on and a little chaotic in the best way: one day we are tuning a drivetrain, the next we are rebuilding a mechanism, testing autonomous paths, or figuring out why a sensor disagrees with everyone else.

Robotics is where we learn to make good decisions with limited time, ask better questions when something fails, and finish work we can stand behind. We take competition seriously, but the people around the robot matter just as much. Students arrive with different experience levels; everyone gets a chance to learn, contribute, and help the next person along.

## What we work on

- Robot software with WPILib, Java, command-based code, autonomous routines, and operator controls
- Mechanical design, fabrication, wiring, pneumatics, and systems testing
- Scouting, match strategy, outreach, business, media, and team operations

## How we use GitHub

This organization is our shared workshop for software, documentation, scouting tools, and team projects. We use issues to record work, pull requests to review changes, and automated builds to catch problems before they reach the robot.

If you are a team member, start with the repository's README and contribution guide. Keep changes focused, describe what you tested, and ask for help early. A clean commit is nice; a safe, understood robot is better.

## Follow along

Our public repositories show the parts of our work we are ready to share. During the season, expect frequent changes as the robot evolves from first prototype to competition machine.
